`POST /v2/household-calculations` sends a stored household, under a stored policy or current law, with optional axes, to the simulation service for calculation, and `GET /v2/household-calculations/{calculation_id}` returns its status and, once it succeeded, the calculated household with its warnings and, in the US, the SPM selection applied and its receipt. Neither needs an account; a country whose `countries` row does not allow household calculations is refused with 422.
