PATCH /v2/user-policies/{id} accepts policy_id, so a saved policy can point at another v2 policy in its country while keeping its ID, name and creation time. Supplied alone, policy_id clears the v1 reference (legacy_policy_id); supplied with legacy_policy_id, both are stored, and policy_id must be the v2 copy of that v1 policy when one exists. Unknown, other-country and conflicting policies are refused as on creation, and rows maintained by the v1 saved-policy mirror still return 409.
