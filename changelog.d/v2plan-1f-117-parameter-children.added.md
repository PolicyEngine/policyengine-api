Catalog publication writes each catalog version's parameter tree into a new table, parameter_tree_children, derived from the version's stored parameters and parameter nodes: one row per node and parameter under its parent's path, with its label, its position among its siblings, its direct child counts, and whether a reform can change it. A bracket schedule is one node whose children are its brackets, labelled "Bracket 1", "Bracket 2" and so on, and labels take the form the app's v3 workspace shows, with a path without a stored label getting one made from the last part of its name. Publication writes the tree of each version it publishes, and its evidence lists them; versions stored before this change keep having no tree.
