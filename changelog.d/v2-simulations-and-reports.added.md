Add `/v2/reports` and `/v2/simulations`: create economy reports and simulations through the simulation service, read a report with its newest run, that run's economy result and the versions the Public API runs, list up to 25 named reports, and read a simulation with its newest run. A create answers 503 only when the simulation service was never reached, and 502 when it may have created the record (its failure, an answer lost after sending, a proxy's 502 or 504, or an unreadable answer), so a caller is not invited to submit a second one; a created record that cannot be read back answers 502 with its `report_id` or `simulation_id`. Saved reports and simulations now also take a detailed-save `report_id` or `simulation_id`, but not together with a v1 number.
