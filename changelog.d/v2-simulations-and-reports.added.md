Add `/v2/reports` and `/v2/simulations`: create economy reports and simulations through the simulation service, read a report with its newest run, that run's economy result and the versions the Public API runs, list up to 25 named reports, and read a simulation with its newest run. A create answers 422 when the simulation service refuses the caller's data: its 422, or its 400 with code `unknown_region`, `unknown_dataset` or `unknown_dynamic`. It answers 502 when the simulation service may have created the record but gave no usable answer: its 500, an answer lost after the request may have reached it, a proxy's 502 or 504 in its place, a 503 that cannot be read or that names only another record, or a created answer that cannot be read. For all but the last, the message asks the caller to submit again and says that this creates a second record if the first request went through. A created record that cannot be read back also answers 502, with its `report_id` or `simulation_id`. It answers 503, and the simulation service has created nothing, when the request was never sent (no connection made, no gateway token, or the simulation service not configured); when the simulation service answers a readable 503 naming no record, a 400 with any other code or none, a 400 or 422 that cannot be read, or any other status; and when the v2 database is not configured or cannot be read for the checks. When the simulation service's 400, 422, 500 or 503 can be read, the answer gives its reason and, when it has one, its code; and a 503 for a record the service created and whose run it then failed gives that record's `report_id` or `simulation_id`. Saved reports and simulations now also take a detailed-save `report_id` or `simulation_id`, but not together with a v1 number.
