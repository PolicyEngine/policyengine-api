UK economy requests now answer 400 for a region .py's UK region registry can't resolve, such as `constituency`, `parliamentary_constituency` or `constituency/all`, with a message that names the region and the accepted forms. The annual and budget-window routes, including local-area breakdowns, check the region before they store anything or submit a simulation. Before, such a request was submitted, failed in the simulation service, and its stored result stayed `computing`. The check applies the simulation service's normalisation, so area names, lower-case GSS codes and bare country names keep working, and it follows the installed bundle: constituencies and local authorities resolve only when .py's registry lists them. `country/ni` is still accepted and sent as `country/northern_ireland`. US requests are unchanged.
