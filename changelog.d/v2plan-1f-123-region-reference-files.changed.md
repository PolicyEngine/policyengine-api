Catalog publication reads regions from the Public API's checked-in reference files, one per country, instead of PolicyEngine.py's region registry. Each region takes its dataset from the certified data release and its filter from a scoping rule: a row filter on state_fips, congressional_district_geoid or the UK region column, or a weight_replacement filter naming the release's constituency or local-authority weight matrix and the region's ONS code. The UK now publishes 1,015 regions, with constituencies and local authorities by ONS code and Northern Ireland as country/northern_ireland; the national regions are "United States" and "United Kingdom". US places are listed in the reference file but left out of the catalog until they can run, and the publication evidence counts them.
