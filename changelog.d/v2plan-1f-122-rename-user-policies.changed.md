The v2 table user_policies is renamed to user_policy_associations, matching the other user-association tables; its constraints and convention-named indexes take the new name. Saved policies, their v1 mappings and the reports that name them are kept. A temporary view named user_policies, with the runtime role's privileges, lets the release from before the rename keep reading and writing saved policies during the deploy and after a rollback; a later change removes it. The privilege reconciliation now finds, grants and verifies privileges on views. The route /v2/user-policies, the user_policy_id columns, legacy_user_policy_mappings and the v1 Cloud SQL table user_policies are unchanged.
